Artist Biography

The story so far

Trazy Jay Rsa is an Afro-Pop and Hip Hop recording artist and songwriter from Ntumbane, Pongola, in KwaZulu-Natal. Born on 3 March 2002 at Etsheni Lejuba Hospital, he grew up surrounded by the sounds and storytelling traditions of KZN — an influence that still runs through everything he records.

Signed to Last Gido Entertainment, he writes music built on memorable melodies, modern African rhythms and lyrics that don't dress up the truth. He moves easily between Afro-Pop and Hip Hop without losing the thread of his own sound — a voice shaped by where he's from and where he's headed.

As both recording artist and songwriter, Trazy Jay treats every track as its own piece of writing. Ambition, relationships, real life, the climb of a young artist building a name — the subject changes, the honesty doesn't.

He's part of a new wave of KZN talent with the energy and the songwriting to travel — from Pongola to stages across South Africa and beyond.
